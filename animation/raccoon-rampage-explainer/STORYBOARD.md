# Raccoon Rampage: How to Play (storyboard)

A 2 minute 54 second animated explainer for *BioInvaders! Raccoon Rampage*.
Sources: the Jan 2024 rulebook (factual source), the ecologygames_raccoon site and
paidia.fun (context and tone), and the game's box and card art (visual reference only).

**Format:** 1920x1080, 30 fps, music at 120 BPM (1 bar = 2 s = 60 frames).
**Look:** hand-painted picture book. Warm paper, watercolour washes painted with
p5.brush, dark plum ink outlines, and a palette taken from the game (plum, lavender,
cream, charcoal). Each role has a colour: Germany mustard, France blue, Animal Rights
pink, Hunter green. The Impact Tracker runs from green to red.
**Text:** short captions in the game's own heading font (Monthoers). The pictures
carry the explanation.

## Cast
- **Rascal**: the raccoon who stars in the film. He is cheeky and greedy and acts out
  the invasion. He appears with sunglasses and a tie as the "Spread" boss.
- **Four players** (bust portraits with role props):
  German Env Agency (yellow, clipboard), French Env Agency (blue, binoculars),
  Animal Rights Activist (pink, paw placard), Hunting Lobbyist (green, feathered hat).
- **Game pieces**: vote cubes, raccoon tokens (yellow/blue/black), policy, action,
  event and spread cards, hunting die, and the Impact Tracker marker.

## Transitions
- Landscape to map: the camera tilts up and the painted hills flatten into the map.
- Title to board: a tail swipe paints across the frame.
- Scenes on the board: continuous camera moves between queue, map, tracker and storyline,
  so the board itself connects the explanation.
- Evaluation fail to Spread: the crumpled card bursts into the spread deck.
- Rules to endings: a painted brush-stroke wipe.

## Sound
Music is synthesised to fit the scenes: a sneaky pizzicato intro, a bouncy marimba theme
for the rules, tension for Spread, a fanfare for the win and a wah-wah for the loss.
Sound effects are timed to on-screen events (card flips, cube clacks, dice, raccoon chirps).
