# Raccoon Rampage: how to play (animated explainer)

A 3-minute, text-light animated explainer for **BioInvaders! Raccoon Rampage** (Paidia, 2024),
based on the January 2024 rulebook plus raccoonrampage.ecologygames.eu and paidia.fun.

- 1920 x 1080, 24 fps, 180 s (4320 frames). Music at 120 BPM: 1 bar = 2 s = 48 frames.
- Style: hand-painted on cream paper. Ink outlines (p5.brush), watercolour glazes, the game's own
  palette (plum/charcoal board, cream cards, dusty pink, player colours) and its title font (Monthoers).
- Everything happens on, above or around one painted game board so scenes flow by camera moves.
- No narration. Short captions (2 to 6 words) carry the rules; pictures do the rest.

## Rules facts shown (from the rulebook, Jan 2024)

- 3-4 players, 5 rounds, each round = 5 years; each round opens with a Storyline Event card (scenario: Big Tech Rules Europe begins with Corporate Relief).
- Setup: 10 yellow raccoon tokens on Germany, 5 blue on France, 5 black on green squares; Impact Tracker on neutral.
- Influence: German and French agencies 3, Animal Rights and Hunter 1 (votes per turn).
- Turn = Queue phase (queue advances one space right, front card leaves and is evaluated, card entering space 4 flips face up) then Main phase (add a policy face down at the back, place votes equal to influence, optional action cards, optional discard 1, draw back up to 5).
- Evaluation: votes on the card >= cost at top right means it passes and its effects trigger; the player with most votes decides where effects happen (owner breaks ties). Not enough votes: Spread.
- Mitigate Impact / Reduce Population remove a token and move the tracker one space toward green. Mitigated tokens from France or Germany go to that agency's score track; population reduction goes to the Hunter's track. Paw / crosshair icons give Animal Rights / Hunter a cube on their score track when a policy passes.
- Spread: cards dealt face down to France, Germany, Rest of Europe; how many depends on the furthest colour reached (green 1, orange 2, red 3 at level 1). Spread-protection cubes cancel a card before reveal. Each token added moves the tracker one space toward red. Rest-of-Europe tokens fill green, then orange, then red squares.
- Tracker reaching the skull ends the game immediately and everyone loses. After round 5: end-game evaluation of face-up queue cards (failures cause no spread). Tracker neutral or red: everyone loses. Green: highest individual score wins; ties share the win.

## Game states (one rules-consistent 4-player game)

Turn order is Germany, France, Animal Rights, Hunter, so a round is 4 turns. All states are
defined in `src/board.js` as `RR.board.SETUP` and `RR.board.STATES.*`; scenes must use them.

| State | When | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| `SETUP` | s03, s04 | Inaction in space 1; Scare Crows!, No More Pets, Hobbyist Hunting face up in 2-4; Protect, Burgers, Drones, Wear face down in 5-8; no votes; tokens DE 10, FR 5, rest 5; tracker 0; no protection cubes |
| `S5` | end of s05 | SETUP + Corporate Relief revealed (round 1) |
| `MID` | start of s06 (after a "time passes" flurry) | turn 5 (round 2, Germany): Corporate Self Interest revealed; queue Protect [DE DE DE FR AR], Burgers [corp], Drones [FR], Wear [HU corp], 4 face-down (virus, bins, 2 more); tokens DE 10, FR 6, rest 7 (orange reached); tracker +3; protection DE + FR |
| `S6` | end of s06 | queue advanced: Protect on EVALUATE; Burgers, Drones, Wear, Raccoon Virus (just flipped) in 1-4; space 8 empty |
| `S7` | end of s07 | Protect passed: 2 German tokens mitigated (DE 8); tracker +1 |
| `S8` | end of s08 | "Next turn" (turn 6, France): Burgers failed 1 of 3; spread FR +0, DE +1, rest +0 +2 after protection cancelled one French and one German card; tokens DE 9, FR 6, rest 9; tracker +4; queue Drones [FR], Wear [HU corp], Virus, Raccoon-Proof Bins (just flipped) |
| `S9` | end of s09 | France placed a face-down policy in space 8 and 3 votes: Drones [FR FR FR], Wear [HU corp FR] |
| `S10` | end of s10 | Behind the Scenes moved corp + FR votes from Wear to Drones: Drones 5/7, Wear [HU] |
| `END` | start of s11 | after five rounds (Big Farm Corp, Free Trade, Europe Burns flip in s11): queue Scare Crows! [FR] fails, Raccoon Virus 7/7 passes, Protect 5/5 passes, No More Pets 2/5 fails; tokens DE 9, FR 6, rest 9; tracker +4 |

Rule points the film must respect: the queue advances at the start of every turn; the card
moving into space 4 flips, and under Corporate Relief a corporate (briefcase) policy gets a
grey vote at that moment; only the active player may play YOUR MAIN PHASE action cards;
the tracker moves one space per token added or removed; reaching the skull ends the game.
